Proposal funnel constructor

Pick the job you're looking at and how you'd write the proposal. Each stage shows your average rate, moved by every trait you select. Effects come from 1,636 of your proposals (July 2024 – September 2026), estimated together so overlapping traits don't double-count. Unselected traits stay at average; pick several options in one trait to average them.

How to read this

  • Sample: 1,636 proposals, 554 opened, 168 replies, 20 hires. Open effects rest on plenty of data; reply effects on 168 replies; hire effects on just 20 hires, so the hire column is a rough guide.
  • Badges: "solid" means the trait passed a false-discovery-rate test (q < 0.1) for that stage, "hint" q < 0.4, "weak" otherwise. Tests control for time period only, so a letter trait can look significant for opens partly because of the jobs it was used on.
  • Effects are shrunk: open and reply effects come from one regularized model per stage with all traits plus time period, so rare options and overlapping traits are pulled toward average. Hire effects are raw hire rates per option, shrunk with 25 pseudo-replies.
  • Combinations are extrapolations: the percentages come from the model, not from past proposals with that exact mix. Use "Trust per stage" to dial effects back if a stack looks too good.
  • Correlation, not causation: these are patterns in your past proposals. Only "no bold" and the job-side drivers of opens are strong findings; the rest are leads to test.
