Proposal funnel constructor

Pick the job and letter traits you care about. Each stage's probability is the baseline rate adjusted by the effects your own data shows for every selected trait, combined as odds ratios. Unselected factors stay at average.

Disclaimers

  • Views are the only solid stage. They rest on 1,635 proposals and several effects that survive the false-discovery correction.
  • Replies are hypotheses. There are only 38 replies, and none of the reply effects survive the false-discovery correction.
  • Hires are directional only. There are just 20 hires, so hire effects come from raw hires per reply and are shrunk hard toward average. They have no significance test.
  • Overlapping traits overstate the result. Many traits overlap (for example startup, long brief and very detailed), but the model multiplies them as if independent. Stacked picks can look too good; use "Trust per stage" to dial effects back toward average.
  • Combinations are extrapolations. The percentages come from the model, not from proposals you actually sent with that exact mix of traits.
  • Small samples are shrunk. Options with few proposals are pulled toward average, so rare options (marked by the small n on each chip) can understate their true effect.
  • Missing data means no effect. Where your sheets had no data for a factor at a stage (for example industry and brief length at the hire stage), it has no effect there.
  • Traits were labelled by an AI from the text alone. Job and letter traits were tagged by an LLM reading each proposal without knowing the outcome, so some labels will be noisy or inconsistent.
  • Correlation, not causation. Effects describe patterns in your past proposals, not guaranteed results, and may drift over time.
